About these health guides

Doctor24 health guides are intended to provide general educational information and help readers understand possible next steps. They do not replace an in-person examination when physical assessment, testing, urgent treatment or other clinical evaluation is required.

Where indicated, articles are authored or medically reviewed by Dr. Bhagirath Vagh, MBBS General Physician.

Important: Doctor24 is not an emergency service. If symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening or potentially life-threatening, seek immediate in-person emergency medical care.
