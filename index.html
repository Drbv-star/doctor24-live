Questions, answered.

Is Doctor24 available 24×7?

Consultation requests can be made 24×7. Actual consultation availability and response time may vary, and a slot will be confirmed with you.

Can I consult from anywhere in India?

Online consultations can be provided when telemedicine is clinically appropriate and applicable requirements are followed.

Will I always get a prescription?

No. The doctor decides whether a prescription, advice, investigation, referral or in-person examination is appropriate.

What if my problem is an emergency?

Doctor24 is not an emergency service. For severe or life-threatening symptoms, contact your nearest emergency department or local emergency service immediately.

Not sure what to do next?

Start with a conversation with a doctor. Online consultation is available for appropriate non-emergency medical concerns.
